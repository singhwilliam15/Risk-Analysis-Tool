**Subject:** Request for permission: non-commercial academic research using NSE public disclosures

Dear Sir / Madam,

I am a PGDM (Banking & Financial Services) student at IMI Delhi. I am building an open-source, non-commercial risk
research tool on Indian listed companies
([github.com/singhwilliam15/Risk-Analysis-Tool](https://github.com/singhwilliam15/Risk-Analysis-Tool)). It studies
whether public disclosures, such as promoter pledges, auditor events and credit-rating actions, help predict large
share-price falls.

Your Terms of Use prohibit automated data collection, and the redistribution of website content without NSE's prior
written permission. I would like to ask for that permission, for the following use only.

1. **Data:**
   - daily equity bhavcopies;
   - the daily F&O ban and price-band change files;
   - quarterly shareholding-pattern XBRL filings;
   - corporate announcements under Regulation 30 (auditor changes, statements on audit qualifications and
     credit-rating disclosures), with their attachments.

   The period is 2014 to today.
2. **Method:** a script with a clear identity, at no more than one request a second, at off-peak hours if you
   prefer, with a one-time download and small monthly updates. The total stays within the 2 GB research threshold
   mentioned in your disclaimer, if that applies.
3. **Purpose:** academic research and a public portfolio project. There is no commercial use, no resale, and no
   trading or advisory service.
4. **Redistribution:** I would publish only derived statistics, such as test results and counts, with full
   acknowledgement that the underlying data come from NSE, and not the raw files. If you also allow publishing
   small derived datasets (for example, one row per company and quarter, with the pledged percentage), please let
   me know the conditions.

If an official data product or research channel suits this use better, I would be glad to use it instead.

Thank you for considering this request.
